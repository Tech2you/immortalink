TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Data & access

Know where the numbers come from.

Private owner access

Family managers and other app members cannot open this dashboard. Access is checked on every request. Customer photos, recordings, messages and memory text are never loaded here.

What is measured

Accounts and families
Current registered accounts and families with an included member. Explicit test exclusions apply. Real activity is not inferred from registration.
Paying contributors
Distinct purchasers with a currently active production plan, an unexpired paid period and a verified positive Apple transaction price. Free offers, expired plans and test purchases are excluded. Unclassified plans are flagged.
Monthly active users
Not available yet. Requires a future app update to record limited activity events without memory contents.
Daily history
One database snapshot each day at 00:10 UTC. Refresh shows current totals; the chart remains daily.
MRR and churn
Entered from your production reports. MRR is not the amount Apple transfers to your bank. No historic churn is invented from today’s active plans.
Costs and results
Imported Apple financial proceeds and entered invoices. Exchange rates are supplied by you. Results exclude income tax, unrecorded costs and accounting adjustments.

Exclude testing from business counts

These exclusions affect this dashboard only. They do not change accounts, subscriptions, media or access in Heiro. Stored test files still count towards actual storage usage.
