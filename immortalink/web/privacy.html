Ever Roots Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026

Ever Roots helps families create private family trees, vaults, memories, photos, voice notes, and AI-assisted memory conversations. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

During TestFlight beta testing, Ever Roots is still being tested with invited users. Please only upload content you have the right to share with the family members you invite.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication user ID.
  • Profile and family tree information, such as names, display names, relationships, family groups, and invite status.
  • User content you choose to add, including memories, written answers, photos, audio recordings, voice notes, and related metadata.
  • AI interaction data, such as prompts, selected memory context, generated responses, transcripts, and usage counters.
  • Technical information needed to run the app, such as session state, upload paths, error logs, device/browser details, and security events.
  • Subscription and entitlement status when paid features are enabled. Payments are processed by Apple; we do not receive your full payment card details.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account, vault, family trees, invites, and memories.
  • To store and display the photos, voice notes, and memories you upload.
  • To provide AI-assisted features, such as memory chat, suggested prompts, transcription, and family context.
  • To enforce free, beta, and paid usage limits, including storage, uploads, invites, and AI usage.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.
  • To manage subscriptions and access to paid features when App Store billing is enabled.

Family Sharing And Invites

Ever Roots is built around family sharing. If you join or create a family group, other authorized family members may be able to see shared family tree information, vault content, memories, photos, voice notes, and AI-assisted content depending on their access. Do not invite people or upload content unless you are comfortable sharing it within that family space.

AI Features

Ever Roots uses AI services to help generate memory responses, conversation prompts, and voice-note transcriptions. AI responses can be inaccurate or incomplete. Do not use AI responses as legal, medical, financial, or emergency advice.

Service Providers

We use trusted service providers to operate Ever Roots, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, Edge Functions, and related logs.
  • OpenAI for AI-assisted chat, embeddings, and transcription features.
  • Apple for TestFlight, App Store distribution, subscriptions, purchases, and app review.

These providers process information only as needed to provide, secure, and improve Ever Roots and their services.

Storage, Security, And Retention

We use access controls, private storage buckets, authentication, database security policies, and usage limits to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use strong passwords and only invite people you trust.

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Ever Roots, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain backups. If you delete your account or content, we will delete or de-identify it where reasonably possible, subject to backups and legal requirements.

Your Choices

  • You can choose what memories, photos, voice notes, and family details you add.
  • You can remove content from the app where deletion tools are provided.
  • You can request account deletion from inside the app.
  • You can manage App Store subscriptions through Apple once paid subscriptions are enabled.
  • You can contact us to ask questions about your data.

Children And Family Content

Ever Roots is not directed to children under 13. Adults may add family memories or family tree information that includes children or relatives. You are responsible for making sure you have the right permissions to upload and share that content.

International Use

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than where you live. We use service providers and safeguards intended to protect information according to this policy.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Ever Roots changes. If we make important changes, we will update the effective date and may provide additional notice in the app or through App Store/TestFlight updates.

Contact Us

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@everroots.app.
