Changes
- Relationship shortcut matches explicit viewer-to-profile questions only.
  Story questions mentioning relatives go through grounded source retrieval.
- Relationship context uses confirmed graph links, not family roles/slots.
  Bounded recorded-relative counts distinguish the profile from the viewer.
  Missing, failed and truncated graph results are handled conservatively.
- Prompts define my/your perspective and prohibit invented relationships,
  favourites, events and emotions. Source text is treated as untrusted data.
- Empty profiles return a specific missing-information answer without a model
  call. Zero recorded relatives means no linked profiles, not nonexistence.
- Icebreaker suggestions require source evidence. Missing-data responses are
  concise instead of returning a generic list of topics.
- Both AI chat screens keep the keyboard and next-message draft intact while
  waiting, scroll only to the real boundary, and respect reading older messages.
- Current gpt-4o-mini model, 220-token answer cap, bounded retrieval and paid
  usage/consent/access checks remain in place. Timeout timers are cleared.
